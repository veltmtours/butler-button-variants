Travel alerts

When travel goes wrong, your guest hears it from you

Butler watches each guest's arrival and departure flights and the weather around your property, and tells them early, with a way to get help.

  • Flight watch

    Add arrival and departure flight numbers with the guest list. Butler checks live status 24 hours, 6 hours, 3 hours and 90 minutes before departure, then every 30 minutes once a flight is late, and messages the guest if it is delayed, cancelled or diverted.

  • Weather warnings

    National Weather Service warnings for your area arrive as a ready-to-send alert. You choose which guests get it. (US properties.)

  • Help in one word

    Guests reply ASSIST and our team picks it up: a rebooked car, a late arrival, an extra night.

  • A car that follows the flight

    We work with a local limo partner near your property. When a flight is delayed or cancelled, the pickup moves with it. If the flight is diverted to a nearby airport, we arrange the pickup from that airport to your hotel.

  • See trouble coming

    Your Alerts tab lists upcoming guest flights with live status, the weather at each airport and an early-warning risk band, so the desk knows who may arrive late.

  • Considerate by design

    Each alert is sent once, quiet hours are respected, and guests can reply STOP at any time.

Alerts go by text message to US and Canadian mobile numbers, and only to guests who have activated Butler from their welcome email.
